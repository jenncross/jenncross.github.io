How to explore LOWESS
→ Drag Reveal Fit to watch LOWESS build its curve one local fit at a time.
→ Adjust frac — small = wiggly, large = smooth.
→ Try frac=1.0 to see it approach ordinary linear regression.
→ Add outlier points and see how LOWESS handles them locally.
How to explore Polynomial
→ Start at degree 1 (line), then increase — count the bends.
→ Crank degree to max and watch it oscillate — that’s overfitting.
→ Toggle Residuals on to see the error at each point.
→ Switch back to LOWESS on the same data to compare.
